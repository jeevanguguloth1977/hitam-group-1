Process

From first sketch to final fit

A typical project follows four stages. The order stays the same whether it is one shower door or a whole floor of partitions.

  1. 1

    Brief

    Tell us the room, the opening and the look you want. Photos and sketches help.

  2. 2

    Measure

    Site measurements fix the size of every frame and pane before anything is cut.

  3. 3

    Make

    Aluminium frames are finished in your chosen colour and matched with the glass.

  4. 4

    Fit

    Installed straight and level, checked, and handed over clean.
